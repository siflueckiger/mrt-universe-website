# TG53 aka Gähn Zzzz Bäng

## aka puzzle palooza

**Format:** Video works for an art installation
**Videos:** <https://tg53.netlify.app/>
**Documentation:** <https://puzzle-palooza.netlify.app/dokumentation/>

---

One project, two addresses. The **videos** are the work itself: short pieces
made for the installation, collected on their own site. The **puzzle palooza
documentation** is the other half — the record of how it was built, what it
was for, and what happened around it.

- **Videos** — [watch the works](https://tg53.netlify.app/)
- **Documentation** — [read the documentation](https://puzzle-palooza.netlify.app/dokumentation/)

> Two doors into the same room.

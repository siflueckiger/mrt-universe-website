# TG53 aka Gähn Zzzz Bäng

## aka puzzle palooza

**Format:** Physical puzzle box for two players
**Documented:** Open Education Day — Bern, 6 May 2023

---

A box full of modules, built for two people who have to talk to each other.
The idea started as a project for a school class, sparked by *Keep Talking and
Nobody Explodes*.

It is not a bomb. It is a **dream generator** — and it has to be shut down
before everyone is left with nothing but nightmares. Two players, several
modules, one conversation that has to work.

- **Two players** — nobody solves it alone
- **Several modules** — each one a different kind of riddle
- **Shut it down** — before the dreams turn

> Not a bomb. A dream generator.

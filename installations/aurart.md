# AurART

## Aura photo installation

**Venue:** Museum für Kommunikation — Bern
**Format:** Aura photo installation
**Documented:** 9 November 2023

---

Sit down and a halo of colour gathers around your body: _AurART_ turns the
space around you into a vivid **aura portrait** — part photograph, part
energy field.

- **One of a kind** — every portrait is different
- **Made on the spot** — the picture appears while you are still sitting there
- **Follows the body** — the colours move when you move

> A self-portrait made of your own electricity!?

## How it actually works

No bio-feedback, no voltage, no mysticism. A script watches where people are
standing in the camera frame and paints a colourful splash right there. The
"aura" is a drawing of your position — which is why it never lands on the same
body twice.

It looks like a portrait of an invisible field. It is a camera, a computer and
a very good costume.

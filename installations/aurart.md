# AurART

## Aura photo installation

**Venue:** Museum für Kommunikation — Bern
**Format:** Aura photo installation

---

Your energy becomes an image. _AurART_ reads the small electrical signals
of the body and turns them into a vivid **aura portrait** — part photograph,
part energy field.

- **Bio-feedback** — the body becomes the pigment
- **One of a kind** — every portrait is unique
- **Made in public** — the picture appears while you are still sitting there

> A self-portrait made of your own electricity!?

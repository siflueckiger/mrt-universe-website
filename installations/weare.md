# WeAre!

## An alien view on the human population

**Venue:** Sockel — Bern
**Format:** VR installation
**Documented:** 30 September 2018

---

Visitors put on VR goggles and see themselves from above. Watched from up
there, the everyday turns strange: how we greet each other, how close we
stand, what we do with our hands while talking.

For a few minutes you are the one taking notes, not the one being studied.

- **Interactive** — visitors become part of the observation
- **Above the room** — you watch yourself from the outside
- **Playful** — a light-hearted mirror held up to humanity

> What would an extraterrestrial write in its field notes about us?

## From WeAre to WeAre.U

Later the idea grew into **WeAre.U**: UWB tags track the players, who see
themselves as abstract points from a bird's-eye view — the body reduced to a
coordinate.

# WeAre!

## An alien view on the human population

**Venue:** Sockel — Bern
**Format:** Interactive installation
**Documented:** 30 September 2018

---

What does humanity look like from the outside? *WeAre!* hands the visitor a
pair of alien eyes and asks for field notes on the species in the room.

The things we never question — how we greet each other, how close we stand,
what we do with our hands while talking — turn into exotic behaviour once the
observer stops being human. The installation makes that shift of perspective
the whole point: you are no longer the one being studied.

- **Interactive** — visitors become part of the observation
- **Playful** — a light-hearted mirror held up to humanity
- **A change of scale** — one room, an entire population

> What would an extraterrestrial write in its field notes about us?

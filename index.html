Welcome to the Magic Ramba Trash Universe

HOW TO PLAY:

  • Use the ARROW KEYS or the JOYSTICK to navigate and discover links.
  • ENTER or the ACTION BUTTON opens links when you're close to one.
  • S or the NEXT LINK BUTTON selects links so you can travel to them quickly.
